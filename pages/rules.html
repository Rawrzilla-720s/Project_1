Poker hand rankings

Used in Texas Hold'em, Omaha and Five Card Draw · Best hand first

  1. Royal flush

    Ace, king, queen, jack and ten, all one suit.

    AA KK QQ JJ 1010

  2. Straight flush

    Five cards in a row, all one suit.

    99 88 77 66 55

  3. Four of a kind

    All four cards of one rank.

    QQ QQ QQ QQ 44

  4. Full house

    Three of a kind with a pair.

    KK KK KK 77 77

  5. Flush

    Any five cards of one suit.

    AA JJ 88 55 22

  6. Straight

    Five cards in a row, mixed suits.

    1010 99 88 77 66

  7. Three of a kind

    Three cards of one rank.

    88 88 88 KK 33

  8. Two pair

    Two different pairs.

    JJ JJ 55 55 AA

  9. One pair

    Two cards of one rank.

    1010 1010 KK 77 44

  10. High card

    None of the above. Your highest card plays.

    AA QQ 99 66 33

Betting moves

What you can do when the action reaches you

Check
Pass the action without betting. Only possible when nobody has bet yet this round.
Bet
Put chips in when nobody else has this round.
Call
Match the current bet to stay in the hand.
Raise
Match the current bet and add more on top.
Fold
Give up your cards and anything you have already put in.
All in
Bet every chip you have left.

Texas Hold'em

2–10 players · One 52-card deck · Intermediate

Goal

Win the pot by holding the best five-card hand at the showdown, or by betting until everyone else folds.

Setup

The two players left of the dealer post the small and big blinds. Everyone is dealt two cards face down, called hole cards.

How a round goes

  1. Pre-flop: betting starts with the player left of the big blind.
  2. Flop: three shared cards are dealt face up, then another round of betting.
  3. Turn: a fourth shared card, then betting.
  4. River: a fifth and final shared card, then the last round of betting.
  5. Showdown: players still in the hand reveal their cards.

Winning

The best five-card hand made from any mix of your two hole cards and the five shared cards takes the pot. See which hands win

Omaha

2–9 players · One 52-card deck · Advanced

Goal

Same as Hold'em: finish with the best five-card hand or be the last player betting.

Setup

Blinds are posted as in Hold'em, but every player is dealt four hole cards instead of two.

How a round goes

  1. Pre-flop betting, starting left of the big blind.
  2. Flop: three shared cards, then betting.
  3. Turn: one more shared card, then betting.
  4. River: the final shared card, then the last round of betting.
  5. Showdown.

Winning

Your hand must use exactly two of your hole cards and exactly three of the shared cards. That one rule is what makes Omaha harder to read than Hold'em. See which hands win

Five Card Draw

2–6 players · One 52-card deck · Beginner

Goal

Hold the best five-card poker hand after one chance to improve it.

Setup

Each player puts a small ante in the pot and is dealt five cards face down.

How a round goes

  1. First round of betting.
  2. The draw: each player may throw away up to three cards and take new ones from the deck.
  3. Second round of betting.
  4. Showdown.

Winning

The best hand at the showdown wins the pot. Nobody shares cards, so every hand is private. See which hands win

Blackjack

1–7 players against the dealer · One or more decks · Beginner

Goal

Finish closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

Setup

Number cards count their value, jacks, queens and kings count 10, and an ace counts 1 or 11. Every player and the dealer get two cards; one dealer card stays face down.

How a round goes

  1. Place your bet before the deal.
  2. Look at your two cards and the dealer's face-up card.
  3. Hit to take another card, stand to keep your total, double down to double the bet for one last card, or split a pair into two hands.
  4. Go over 21 and you bust and lose straight away.
  5. The dealer reveals the hidden card and must keep hitting until reaching 17 or more.

Winning

Beat the dealer's total and you win your bet. An ace with a ten-value card is a blackjack and pays 3 to 2. A tie is a push and your bet comes back.

Go Fish

2–6 players · One 52-card deck · Beginner

Goal

Collect the most books. A book is all four cards of one rank.

Setup

Deal seven cards each, or five each with four or more players. The rest go face down in the middle as the pond.

How a round goes

  1. On your turn, ask one player for a rank you already hold, such as “Any sevens?”
  2. If they have any, they hand them all over and you ask again.
  3. If not, they say “Go fish” and you draw from the pond. Draw the rank you asked for and you go again.
  4. Lay down every complete book in front of you.

Winning

The game ends when all thirteen books are made. Most books wins.

Crazy Eights

2–7 players · One 52-card deck · Beginner

Goal

Be the first player with no cards left.

Setup

Deal five cards each, or seven with two players. Turn the top card of the deck face up to start the discard pile.

How a round goes

  1. Play a card that matches the top discard by suit or by rank.
  2. Eights are wild: play one at any time and name the suit the next player must follow.
  3. Can't play? Draw from the deck until you can. If the deck runs out, pass.

Winning

The first player to empty their hand wins the round. Others can score penalty points for the cards they are still holding.

Hearts

4 players · One 52-card deck · Intermediate

Goal

Finish with the lowest score. Every heart you take is 1 point and the queen of spades is 13.

Setup

Deal thirteen cards each. Before each hand, pass three cards: left, then right, then across, then no pass, and repeat.

How a round goes

  1. Whoever holds the two of clubs leads it.
  2. Follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can't, play anything.
  3. The highest card of the led suit wins the trick and leads the next one.
  4. Hearts can't be led until a heart has been played on another suit.

Winning

Play ends when someone reaches 100 points, and the lowest score wins. Take every heart and the queen of spades in one hand and you shoot the moon: you score zero and everyone else takes 26.

Gin Rummy

2 players · One 52-card deck · Intermediate

Goal

Turn your hand into melds: sets of three or four of a rank, or runs of three or more in one suit.

Setup

Deal ten cards each. Turn one card face up to start the discard pile and leave the rest face down as the stock.

How a round goes

  1. Draw the top card of the stock or the discard pile.
  2. Discard one card face up.
  3. Knock when your unmatched cards add up to 10 or less. Face cards count 10 and aces count 1.
  4. Go gin by knocking with no unmatched cards at all.

Winning

The knocker scores the difference between both players' unmatched cards, plus a bonus for gin. If the other player matches or beats the knocker's count, they undercut and score instead. First to 100 wins.

Solitaire

1 player · One 52-card deck · Beginner

Goal

Move every card onto four foundation piles, one per suit, built from ace up to king.

Setup

Deal seven columns from left to right, holding one to seven cards, with only the top card face up. The rest of the deck is the stock.

How a round goes

  1. Build down the columns in alternating colours, such as a red seven on a black eight.
  2. Move aces to the foundations as they appear, then build each suit upward.
  3. Only a king can fill an empty column.
  4. When you're stuck, turn cards from the stock, one or three at a time.

Winning

You win when all fifty-two cards are on the foundations.
