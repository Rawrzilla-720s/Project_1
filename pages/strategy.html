Strategy

Knowing the rules gets you a seat. These are the habits that help you keep it.

Count your outs

Outs are the cards still in the deck that would turn your hand into a winner. Here is the hand from Table Four.

Your cards

AA KK

The flop

QQ JJ 44

You have four hearts and four cards to a straight. You need one more heart for a flush, or any ten for a straight. The ten of hearts would give you a royal flush.

9 hearts make a flush

22 33 44 55 66 77 88 99 1010

3 more tens make a straight

1010 1010 1010
Outs
129 hearts plus 3 tens. The ten of hearts is only counted once.
Hit by the river
About 48%Rule of 4: with two cards to come, multiply your outs by 4. The exact answer is 45%.
Hit on the next card
About 24%Rule of 2: with one card to come, multiply by 2. The exact answer is 26%.
Is calling 80 worth it?
YesYou pay 80 to win a pot of 600, so you only need to win about 13% of the time. Your draw comes in far more often than that.
